2. Public Helpers, listings and location privacy

“Helpers” or “Uploaders” may post a House, Flat, Shop, Commercial space or PG for rent. Each post requires one to ten auto-compressed photos, an automatically detected or map-pin-selected GPS position, a publicly visible approximate landmark (for example “Near Bagalagunte Bus Stop, ~500 m radius”), owner name, a valid 10-digit Indian mobile number, expected rent, deposit, and short notes.

The exact latitude/longitude and owner phone number are stored privately in Supabase and are hidden behind a server-verified Rewarded Ad unlock or an active Premium entitlement. The public feed, public listing view and public APIs expose only the approximate landmark. GalliRent uses Google Maps in the native Android app; map availability depends on Google services and network access.

Every listing automatically expires after 21 days unless the uploader confirms in the app that it is still vacant. The app may change this configurable period. An expired, paused, closed, deleted, or admin-frozen listing is removed from the public feed.

3. Posting reward, hold and release

Publishing a To-Let post earns the Helper a ₹50.00 posting reward by default (admin-configurable; posts earn nothing when the reward amount is ₹0.00 or the reward is switched off). It applies once per published post to every property type: House, Flat, Shop, Commercial space and PG. GalliRent does not charge Helpers any fee to post.

Hold: the reward is not spendable when the post is published. It is recorded as on hold and released into the Helper's GalliRent wallet only when that post's ad is seen 1,000 times (admin-configurable). An “ad view” is one signed-in user seeing that post's ad — its card in the feed or the opened post — counted at most once per user per post per calendar day. The uploader's own views are never counted, and refreshing or re-opening the same post on the same day does not add another ad view. No claim action is required: the wallet is credited automatically when the target is reached.

Forfeiture: if the post is marked Rented/Closed, deleted, or expires (21 days by default) before the 1,000-ad-view target is reached, the still-held reward is forfeited and is never paid. The posting reward is discretionary promotional spend, not consideration for the property, a broker fee, or a guarantee of earnings. Administrators may release a held reward early, forfeit it, or reverse a credited reward in cases of fake listings, fake phone numbers or photos, duplicate posts, view or reward manipulation, or other abuse.

A released reward counts towards the Helper's wallet balance and the ₹500.00 withdrawal minimum; a reward that is still on hold does not. Posting rewards are separate from the ₹0.05 reward per verified ad unlock and the ₹0.50 honest-closure bonus, and are subject to the same wallet-freeze, anti-fraud and forfeiture rules described below.

4. Ten-minute rewarded-ad unlocks

A free seeker may tap “View Owner Number (Watch Ad for 10 Mins)” or “Exact Google Maps Location (Watch Ad for 10 Mins)”. A Google AdMob Rewarded Video must be completed. GalliRent uses AdMob Server-Side Verification (SSV); a client-side reward animation alone is not sufficient. A verified reward event is consumed once by the secure PostgreSQL RPC unlock_post_feature.

On successful verification, the database records expires_at = NOW() + 10 minutes (or the current admin-configured duration). The app displays a live server-offset countdown such as 09:59, plus Call and WhatsApp buttons for a contact unlock, or Open in Google Maps for a location unlock. When NOW() > expires_at, both buttons automatically re-lock and the private value is removed from the screen. Watching another ad resets the timer to +10 minutes; re-unlocks never generate another uploader payment.

Network latency, app backgrounding, device clock changes, ad-network failures, or delayed SSV callbacks can delay display of the private value. The server expiry is authoritative. Ad-unlocks have no cash value, cannot be paused, transferred, restored, exchanged, extended, or refunded, and are strictly non-refundable once the reward is granted.

5. Uploader wallet and rewards

The wallet ledger records every posting reward paid into the wallet as POST_REWARD_RELEASE. A posting reward that is still on hold is not part of the Helper's balance, so it cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or used to satisfy the ₹500.00 withdrawal minimum until it is released.

The default uploader reward is ₹0.05 (five paise) one time per unique free-user ad unlock for each unique combination of seeker, post and unlock type (owner contact or exact location). The current reward_per_ad_unlock is fetched dynamically from app_settings and may be changed by the administrator. Rewards are credited only after a verified free-user Rewarded Ad completion and only while the listing is ACTIVE, not expired, not paused for review, and the uploader wallet is not frozen.

₹0.00 is credited when a Premium subscriber views contact or exact location, and ₹0.00 is credited on every 10-minute re-unlock, because no new paid unlock event occurred for that same seeker/post/unlock-type combination. Premium views therefore do not generate an ad reward. A reward may be reversed or forfeited if the underlying listing, account, ad event, or report is later found to be fraudulent.

Three unique users reporting a listing as “Already Rented” automatically place the listing in PAUSED_REVIEW, hide it from the feed, and stop uploader earnings until an administrator reviews it. Administrators may approve, pause, edit, delete, or freeze wallets for fake numbers, fake photos, duplicate posts, rented houses left active, or other abuse.

6. Withdrawals and payouts

The Withdraw button unlocks only when the available wallet balance is at least ₹500.00. The maximum withdrawal per request is 75% of the current wallet balance; for a ₹500.00 balance the maximum request is ₹375.00 and 25% remains in the wallet. The app calculates and submits the exact 75% cap server-side; a user cannot choose a higher amount. Only one PENDING payout request may exist at a time.

A Helper must enter a valid UPI ID or bank account number, IFSC and account-holder name. Submitting creates a PENDING payout request for manual administrator verification and UPI/bank transfer within 3–7 working days. GalliRent may request identity or ownership proof, reject an incomplete or suspicious request, and deduct the paid amount from the wallet only when an administrator marks it PAID. The uploader is responsible for taxes, bank charges, incorrect destination details, and applicable law.

We may reject a payout, freeze the wallet, and apply full wallet forfeiture for fake phone numbers, fake or stolen photos, fabricated listings, reward manipulation, or leaving rented houses active. “Reject & Forfeit (Fake Posts)” resets the wallet to ₹0.00 after administrator review and records a balancing forfeiture transaction.

7. Mandatory closure and honesty bonus

An uploader must close or mark a property rented within 24 hours of the property being rented or becoming unavailable. In “My Posts” the uploader must select a mandatory reason (Rented Out, Owner Cancelled, or Wrong Details), use “Call Owner to Confirm”, and tick the confirmation box before closure. The call opens the device dialer; GalliRent does not record call audio.

A configurable honest_closure_bonus, default ₹0.50, may be credited once when an uploader cleanly closes a verified ACTIVE post after the required owner confirmation. No bonus is paid for paused, expired, unverified, duplicate, fake, or already-closed posts, or when the wallet is frozen.

8. Reports and anti-fraud

Repeated false reports, fake listings, fake numbers/photos, ad fraud, automated access, scraping, or attempts to bypass the server-verified timer may result in account termination, wallet forfeiture, and disclosure to authorities where required.
